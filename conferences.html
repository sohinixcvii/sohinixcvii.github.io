About me
Hi! I'm Sohini Dutta, a PhD student at the Jodrell Bank Centre for Astrophysics, University of Manchester. I use Bayesian statistics to try to pull cosmological information from the redshifted 21cm signal from Cosmic Dawn and the Epoch of Reionization out of noisy, systematics-dominated radio data.
